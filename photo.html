Welcome to Longli Lenden Ward

The vibrant commercial heart of Tuli Town, Nagaland — home to 600 warm households, lush rubber farms, and the scenic Milak River.

Explore Our Colony

About Our Colony

Longli Lenden Ward is a bustling, close-knit community comprising approximately 600 households. Serving as the primary commercial hub of Tuli town, our colony beautifully balances vibrant economic activity with peaceful natural landscapes, community fellowship, and strong traditional values.

Community Facilities

Education

We take pride in fostering young minds with our local educational institutions, featuring one private school and one government primary school dedicated to foundational learning.

Faith & Fellowship

Our colony fellowship serves as a peaceful sanctuary for residents to gather, worship God, strengthen community bonds, and share spiritual fellowship.

Governance

As a central part of Tuli town, our ward hosts important administrative infrastructure, including the local PWD offices to support regional development.

Commerce & Nature

Commercial Center

As the main commercial center of Tuli town, our streets are lively with local businesses, shops, and daily trade serving residents and visitors alike.

Rubber Farms

Surrounded by thriving private agricultural ventures, particularly lush rubber farms that contribute significantly to the local green economy.

Milak River Picnic Spot

Flowing right alongside the colony, the pristine Milak River provides a popular getaway for locals and visitors looking to enjoy peaceful picnics and nature.
